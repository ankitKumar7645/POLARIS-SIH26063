Polar Science Fast Facts

Lake Priyadarshini: A pristine freshwater lake in Antarctica, maintained near Maitri station, which stays liquid under thick ice sheets.
The Third Pole: The Himalayas contain the largest volume of ice outside the Polar regions, feeding water to over 1.4 billion people!
Psychrophiles: Microscopic organisms in Antarctica produce natural "antifreeze" proteins to thrive at temperatures below -20°C.
